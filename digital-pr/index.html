Digital PR

Data-led campaigns where one dataset becomes many stories, and every outlet gets an angle written for its readers.

  • Data research

    Building a dataset from public sources and fact-checking it, so every number in the story holds up.

  • Story angles

    Finding the angles in the data and writing a different one for each outlet, so no two journalists get the same story first.

  • Charts

    Briefing shareable charts that make the findings easy to publish.

  • Journalist lists and pitches

    Choosing the right journalists for each angle and writing the pitches, for national, regional and entertainment media.

  • Local markets

    Campaigns written in English first and then adapted to the local language and media of each market.
