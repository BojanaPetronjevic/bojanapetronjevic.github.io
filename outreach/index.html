Outreach

Every email is personalised before it goes out: a real article from the site, a reason the topic fits their readers, and the right language for the market.

  • First contact with a publisher

    A guest post pitch that proves I read the site, offers three ready article angles instead of asking "do you accept guest posts?", and asks about price only once the value is clear.

  • Price negotiation

    Anchors the price to the site's real traffic in its home market, gives the publisher two ways to say yes, and adds a gentle deadline.

  • Follow-up

    Short, adds a new reason to reply such as a timely event, and makes "no" easy, which still clears the lead.

  • Reseller and agency deals

    Sets clear vetting conditions up front, avoids surprises after agreement, and uses steady monthly volume as negotiating leverage.
