How I work

A few principles behind every placement.

  • A small set of trusted individual resellers and no more than two link marketplaces, so quality, prices and delivery stay under control.
  • Every email is personalised: a real article from the site, a reason the topic fits their readers, and the right language for the market.
  • Every publisher is vetted before ordering, so the terms are clear and there are no surprises after agreement.
  • I use AI tools every day and build small tools that remove repetitive work from link building.
